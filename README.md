One-line dad joke entry for the make.fun bounty **"Dad Joke Showdown"**.

`main.py` writes a single one-line dad joke to `/output/entry.txt` — a groan-worthy pun with the punchline at the end, under 200 characters.

The joke (115 characters, one line):

> I tried to teach my dog to fetch the paper, but he kept bringing back dad jokes — turns out he's a groan retriever.
